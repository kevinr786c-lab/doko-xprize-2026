# Product and Business

## Founder motivation

Doko is built by one founder without formal software-engineering training. He
combined prior hospital operations experience with AI-assisted learning after a
period of personal and financial instability. The objective is not rapid growth
at any cost. It is to build a durable business, a dependable service for
clinics, and a better long-term path for his family.

That context explains the product strategy: observe before automating, limit
scope before increasing risk, and make each clinic implementation repeatable
before onboarding many more customers.

## Problem

Independent medical practices often combine messaging, calendars, reception,
public information, supplies, and follow-up through disconnected manual steps.
The doctor may have software, but the clinic still depends on individual memory
and inconsistent front-desk habits.

## Doko's approach

Doko turns observed clinic workflows into a repeatable operational capability:

- The patient receives a clear route to clinic information and scheduling.
- The assistant sees one operational panel and auditable appointment state.
- The doctor keeps control of clinic rules and Google authorization.
- The owner sees platform health separately from normal clinical signals.
- Reusable protocols make onboarding and training less dependent on the founder.
- Suffy extends the relationship into B2B medical supplies and delivery.

## Business model

Current revenue is a monthly subscription paid by participating doctors. The
first ten doctors are planned as a small founder cohort at MXN 1,600 per doctor
per month. After the cohort validates onboarding and support capacity, the
planned standard subscription is MXN 2,500 per doctor per month.

Potential future revenue from Doko Suffy, setup, local presence, or procurement
must not be counted as current recurring revenue until it is actually earned.

## Deliberate growth constraint

Doko is intentionally limiting onboarding while real clinic workflows are
stabilized. This is not presented as unlimited scale. It is a controlled path
to repeatability:

1. Validate reliability and daily use with two clinics.
2. Convert observed work into reusable training and protocols.
3. Add up to ten founder doctors without lowering support quality.
4. Measure support load, retention, usage, and gross margin.
5. Standardize onboarding before broader expansion.

## Evidence already available

- Two paying doctors.
- Three clinic assistants using or supporting the workflow.
- Real patient portal traffic.
- Production appointment volume.
- Cloud Run operation and scheduled-job logs.
- Aggregate AI usage and read-only load testing.

Payment evidence, permissioned testimonials, and exact monthly USD conversion
are maintained outside source control and supplied privately through Devpost.
