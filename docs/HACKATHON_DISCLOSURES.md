# Build with Gemini XPRIZE Disclosures

This document separates the submitted Doko project from resources and
relationships that existed before the program period.

## Submitted project start

- Program period: May 19 through August 17, 2026.
- The founder recalls learning about the hackathon around May 20.
- The earliest preserved integrated Doko design is an Excalidraw scene named
  for May 21 and saved in the early hours of May 22.
- The founder joined Devpost on May 29, as supported by the Devpost welcome
  email. Registration happened after work began, but both dates fall inside the
  official program period.
- Doko, as the submitted AI-enabled operational platform and business, was
  started on May 21, 2026.
- Design, implementation, clinic validation, and the current integrated product
  were completed during the hackathon period.

## Pre-existing resources

The following existed before May 19 and are disclosed as inputs, not as the
submitted project:

- The founder's prior hospital procurement, warehouse, supplier, and clinic
  operations experience.
- A pre-existing relationship with one clinic and its doctor.
- An earlier standalone Google Calendar configuration delivered to that doctor,
  including a payment received before the program period.
- A separate, unsuccessful scheduling/software experiment that provided general
  learning but is not submitted as Doko. Its February-March code and archives
  are excluded from this repository.

The prior Calendar service and abandoned experiments are inputs to the
founder's learning, not claims that the submitted Doko platform existed before

## Built during the hackathon

The submitted work includes the integrated medical panel, patient portals,
confirmation workflow, doko.lat directory and physician pages, Doko Assistant,
AI usage controls, deterministic supervisor, Doko Suffy operations,
role-separated administration, and versioned clinic implementation protocols.

## Team

Doko is built and operated by one founder using AI-assisted development tools.
Clinic doctors and assistants are customers and users, not undisclosed team
members. Their operational feedback informs the product with permission.

Gemini, ChatGPT, Cursor, and OpenAI Codex supported learning, explanation,
design, implementation, and verification. They are development tools, not
human team members. Gemini is the only model used by the production product;
the other tools do not receive production patient or Google Workspace data.
